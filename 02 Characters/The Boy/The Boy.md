# The Boy

## Role

The Boy is the child Madrigal encounters in the snowy park after her shift. His unselfconscious play gives her a moment of connection and helps her recognize the human importance of her unseen work.

The written design below was approved on 2026-11-23. The [canonical visual character sheet](the-boy-character-sheet.png) was generated and accepted that day. Use it together with this written definition as the reference for his design.

## Story Facts

- In the evening, he plays in freshly fallen snow in a park, pretending his coat and sticks are wings and a halo.
- He sees Madrigal, points to her folded wings, and invites her to play without speaking.
- He plays around the shadows cast by her wings, making small footprints in the snow.
- He alternates serious, pondering looks with laughter as they play.
- When his mother calls from a nearby house, he waves goodbye and runs to her, still carrying his stick halo and holding one arm out like a wing.
- His mother is the same mother Madrigal helped earlier that day. She cannot see Madrigal, but smiles toward the park after her son points back at it.

The story does not specify his exact age, facial features, or clothing beyond his coat.

## Canonical Visual Design

These are the approved written design choices:

- He reads as a boy of about five to six years old, with a small, compact build.
- He has a softly rounded, youthful face and fair skin. His cheeks become rosy in the winter cold.
- His large, expressive eyes are amber-brown. He has thick, dark eyebrows, a small rounded nose, and a light scattering of freckles across his nose and cheeks.
- His short, tousled hair is dark chocolate brown and remains visible beneath the falling snow; he wears no hat.
- His silhouette should read clearly as a small child beside Madrigal, an adult. In shared staging, he is approximately waist to chest height relative to her.
- Render him in the film's painterly, handcrafted storybook style. Keep his features natural, youthful, and expressive rather than photorealistic or overly stylized.

## Clothing and Props

His approved winter outfit has a worn, slightly dirty, hand-me-down feel:

- A slightly oversized, mid-thigh-length puffy wool winter coat in muted brick red with warm rust undertones. It has a generous hood, three large dark-brown buttons, rectangular patch pockets, and a gently flared silhouette. The thick, soft fabric is visibly textured, and its loose lower panels can be held outward like wings.
- A thick rust-red knitted wool scarf, loosely wrapped around his neck, with short fringed ends.
- Chunky mustard-yellow knitted gloves with a soft, slightly worn, handmade appearance.
- Loose-fitting dark-navy wool trousers, gathered slightly at the ankles.
- Worn brown leather lace-up winter boots with thick soles.

The improvised stick halo is a story prop, not part of his everyday costume. The coat is the play-wing prop; do not add literal wings or an angel costume.

## Personality and Performance

His play is imaginative and spontaneous. He can pause to study Madrigal with a serious, pondering expression, then break into laughter and movement. Keep the performance natural for a young child, with readable curiosity and warmth rather than exaggerated cartoon acting.

## Relationships and Scale

He is the son of the mother Madrigal helped earlier that day. He can see Madrigal, while his mother cannot. His play with Madrigal is a brief, joyful connection; he should not be portrayed as frightened by her wings.

Keep him distinctly child-sized beside Madrigal's petite adult proportions. His coat and stick halo let him imitate an angel in play, but should not make him look like a miniature version of Madrigal.

## Continuity Requirements

Preserve across shots and all views of a future character sheet:

- Apparent age of about five to six and a small, compact child silhouette.
- Fair skin, rosy winter cheeks, freckles across the nose and cheeks, thick dark eyebrows, amber-brown eyes, and a small rounded nose.
- Short, tousled dark-chocolate-brown hair; no hat.
- The muted brick-red, mid-thigh wool coat with rust undertones, generous hood, three dark-brown buttons, rectangular patch pockets, and gently flared lower panels.
- The matching rust-red scarf, mustard-yellow knitted gloves, navy trousers, and worn brown lace-up boots.
- The same proportions, colors, garment construction, and painterly storybook rendering in front, side, and back views.

When he plays at being an angel, use the coat panels and the stick halo established by the story. Do not add permanent wings or a literal halo. Maintain his clear height difference from Madrigal in shared scenes.
