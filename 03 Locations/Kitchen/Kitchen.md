- Narrow, old, well-used working kitchen; maintained rather than dirty.
- Black stove and cooking line on the left beneath a large dark hood.
- Worn wooden preparation counter on the right and a central working aisle.
- Dark wooden ceiling beams, aged cream tile, copper cookware, open shelves,
  hanging utensils, crockery, and ingredients.
- Warm amber pendant and work lights against cool predawn blue window light.
