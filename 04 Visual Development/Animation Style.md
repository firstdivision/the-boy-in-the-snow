# Animation Style

The film should have the painterly, storybook quality of a handcrafted animated film rather than the finish of photorealistic CGI.

## Core Direction

- Painterly detail with soft edges where appropriate
- A hand-crafted, illustrated quality
- Expressive faces capable of restrained performance
- Watercolor-like snow, fog, and winter atmosphere
- Cool blue shadows throughout the exterior winter world
- Warm golden windows and interior light
- Strong warm/cool contrast as a central visual motif
- Cinematic composition and camera movement

Surfaces and atmosphere should retain visible artistic interpretation. Avoid overly precise materials, lighting, or facial rendering that pushes the images toward realistic CGI.

## Animation Approach

Motion should preserve the source artwork's illustrated character. Favor controlled movement, subtle acting, believable weight, and environmental motion that supports rather than overwhelms the shot.

Madrigal's wings must behave like functional anatomy. Flight should use visible wing-generated lift, slow powerful wingbeats, gliding, feather flex, and coherent body and clothing response—not superhero-style levitation.

Generated atmospheric effects do not need to carry the full visual burden. Snowfall, fog, depth, bloom, and particles may be added or refined in Resolve/Fusion when conventional compositing offers better consistency and control.
