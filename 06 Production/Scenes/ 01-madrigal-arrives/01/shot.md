## Video Prompt

Cinematic establishing shot of Copper Creek on a quiet winter morning just before sunrise.

Maintain the exact composition, character appearance, architecture, lighting, colors, and painterly animated-film style of the reference image.

The entire environment is subtly alive.

Snow falls continuously throughout the entire town and at every depth of the image. Distant snow falls softly over the rooftops and hills. Midground snow drifts naturally between the buildings. Larger soft flakes occasionally cross close to the camera. Snow must move naturally throughout the entire frame and must never appear frozen or painted onto the background.

Thin chimney smoke rises slowly from several houses and drifts gently sideways in the cold morning air. Warm window lights remain steady. The distant predawn sky changes only imperceptibly.

Madrigal flies naturally above Copper Creek, traveling smoothly forward across the town. Her flight is calm, graceful, and effortless.

Her large feathered wings move anatomically and naturally. One slow, powerful wingbeat is followed by a long gentle glide. Her body remains relaxed and streamlined rather than stiff or superhero-like. Her legs trail naturally behind her with relaxed knees and feet.

Her coat hem, scarf, loose strands of dark hair, satchel, and outer wing feathers respond subtly to the airflow.

Madrigal is commuting to work. She is not performing or making a dramatic entrance. Her movement should feel routine to her even though the image itself is beautiful.

The camera performs an extremely slow, smooth cinematic forward drift over the town, creating subtle parallax between foreground rooftops, Madrigal, the town, and distant mountains.

No sudden camera movement. No dramatic banking. No diving. No hovering. No acrobatics. No additional flying characters. No new people or objects entering the foreground. Do not alter Madrigal's clothing, face, wings, or proportions.

Quiet, restrained, painterly animated-film realism.

Duration approximately 5 seconds.
