# Shot 01B — Madrigal in Flight

## Status

Starting Frame Approved

## Estimated Duration

~5 seconds

## Purpose

Introduce Madrigal more clearly after the wide establishing shot.

Show her flying naturally above Copper Creek while establishing an important
aspect of her character: although her existence is extraordinary to us,
flying to work is completely ordinary to her.

This is her morning commute.

## Composition

Medium-wide aerial shot.

Camera is behind and slightly beside Madrigal, close enough to clearly see her
appearance, clothing, wings, satchel, and body language.

Madrigal occupies the left side of the frame while Copper Creek stretches
beneath and ahead of her.

The town remains recognizable as the same Copper Creek established in Shot 01A.

## Time / Lighting

Predawn / very early morning.

Cool blue winter light dominates the landscape, contrasted with warm golden
windows and streetlights below.

A subtle warm glow is beginning to appear along the horizon.

## Camera

Camera travels smoothly with Madrigal from behind and slightly to one side.

Movement is stable and cinematic, as though the camera is flying alongside her.

Very subtle forward movement and natural environmental parallax.

No orbiting around Madrigal.

No sudden camera movement.

No dramatic zoom.

## Action

Madrigal flies calmly toward the center of Copper Creek.

Her flight is practiced, graceful, and effortless.

She performs one slow, natural wingbeat followed by a relaxed glide.

Her legs trail naturally behind her with relaxed knees and feet.

Her coat, scarf, loose strands of hair, satchel, and outer wing feathers move
gently in the airflow.

Madrigal is slightly tired.

She may give a very small yawn or tired exhale while continuing to fly.

The performance should be subtle rather than comedic.

She does not look unhappy.

She simply looks like someone beginning another workday.

## Character

Madrigal.

Maintain her canonical appearance:

- young adult woman
- dark hair gathered loosely into a messy bun
- youthful facial features
- dark blue-gray winter coat
- thick scarf
- winter boots
- brown leather work satchel
- large natural feathered wings

Her proportions should remain consistent with the canonical Madrigal design.

She should appear slender and normally proportioned, not unusually short or
childlike.

## Mood / Tone

Beautiful, quiet, slightly wistful.

There is a gentle visual joke underneath the beauty:

The audience is watching an angel majestically fly over a snow-covered town.

Madrigal is commuting to work.

Do not exaggerate the joke.

## Video Prompt

Cinematic medium-wide aerial tracking shot of Madrigal flying above Copper
Creek on a quiet winter morning just before sunrise.

Maintain the exact character appearance, clothing, wings, proportions,
architecture, lighting, colors, composition, and painterly animated-film style
of the reference image.

Madrigal flies calmly and naturally forward above the town. Her flight is
graceful, practiced, and effortless rather than heroic or dramatic. She
performs one slow, anatomically natural and powerful wingbeat, followed by a
long relaxed glide.

Her body remains streamlined but relaxed. Her legs trail naturally behind her
with slightly relaxed knees and feet rather than remaining stiff. Her arms hang
naturally and respond subtly to her movement.

Her coat hem, scarf, loose strands of dark hair, leather satchel, and outer
wing feathers move continuously and subtly in the airflow.

Madrigal looks slightly tired as she begins another workday. During the glide,
she gives a very small natural yawn or tired exhale, then continues looking
toward the town ahead. The expression is understated and human, not exaggerated
or comedic.

Snow falls naturally throughout the entire scene at multiple depths. Distant
snow moves across the town and mountains, midground flakes pass between
Madrigal and the rooftops, and occasional larger soft flakes cross near the
camera. Snow must not appear frozen in the background.

Chimney smoke rises slowly from buildings below and drifts gently with the
winter air. The town remains otherwise quiet.

The camera flies smoothly alongside Madrigal, positioned behind and slightly
to one side of her. Maintain a stable cinematic tracking movement with subtle
parallax in the town and mountains below.

No sudden camera movement. No diving. No dramatic banking. No acrobatics. No
hovering. No superhero pose. No additional flying characters. Do not add
objects to Madrigal's hands. Do not change her face, clothing, wings, hairstyle,
satchel, or body proportions.

Quiet, restrained, painterly animated-film realism.

Duration approximately 5 seconds.

## Sound

- subtle airflow around Madrigal
- quiet feather movement
- one soft, substantial wingbeat

Music continues from Shot 01A.

or action-movie sound effect.

The visual match should make it immediately clear that the distant flying
figure in Shot 01A is Madrigal.

Cut to Shot 01C as Madrigal approaches her destination.

## Generation Notes

Watch specifically for:

- stiff legs during flight
- unnatural wing articulation
- wings changing size or feather structure
- satchel disappearing or changing sides
- Madrigal becoming too young or too short
- excessive facial animation
- exaggerated yawning
- frozen background snowfall
- camera orbiting around Madrigal
- unintended additional characters
- changes to Copper Creek architecture

Prefer subtle performance over ambitious motion.

If the yawn causes facial or body artifacts, regenerate without the yawn and
use only a small tired exhale or neutral tired expression.

This shot is intentionally similar to the successful sustained-flight proof of
concept.

The important new element is not flight itself but characterization.

By the end of Shot 01B, the audience should understand something about Madrigal
without dialogue:

**She is an angel. She can fly. And she is on her way to work.**
