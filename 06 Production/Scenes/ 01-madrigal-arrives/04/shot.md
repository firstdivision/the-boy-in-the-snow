# Shot 01D — Getting Ready for Work

## Status

Starting Frame

## Estimated Duration

~5 seconds

## Purpose

Humanize Madrigal and establish her attitude toward her work.

She has just completed a beautiful flight over Copper Creek and landed beside
the restaurant.

Now the magical moment gives way to something wonderfully ordinary:

Madrigal gets herself ready for work.

This is the supernatural equivalent of someone getting out of the car in the
employee parking lot, straightening their clothes, taking a breath, and heading
inside.

## Composition

Medium shot.

Madrigal stands in the snowy service alley near the restaurant entrance.

The camera sees her from approximately waist or thigh level upward, while
leaving enough room around her for her wings to remain visible.

The warm restaurant service entrance is visible in the background or to one
side of the frame.

Cold blue morning light surrounds Madrigal while warm golden restaurant light
spills into the alley.

## Time / Lighting

Predawn / early winter morning.

Cool blue ambient winter light.

Warm golden light from the restaurant.

Continuous gentle snowfall.

## Camera

Mostly static medium shot.

A very slow, almost imperceptible cinematic push toward Madrigal is acceptable.

No orbit.

No handheld movement.

No dramatic camera motion.

## Action

Madrigal has just landed.

Her wings finish settling behind her.

She straightens her scarf with one simple movement.

She adjusts the strap of her work satchel on her shoulder.

She briefly brushes a little snow from her coat.

Then she pauses.

Madrigal looks toward the restaurant entrance.

She takes one small breath as if mentally preparing herself for another day.

Her expression says:

"All right. Here we go."

She does not speak.

The performance is subtle and natural.

## Character

Madrigal.

Maintain continuity with the previous shots:

- youthful animated facial design
- dark hair loosely gathered into a messy bun
- dark blue-gray winter coat
- thick scarf
- winter boots
- brown leather work satchel
- large natural feathered wings
- normal young-adult proportions

Madrigal should remain visibly stylized and animated rather than
photorealistic.

## Wings

Her wings are no longer being used for flight.

They begin somewhat open from the landing and naturally relax closer to her
back during the shot.

Wing movement should be subtle.

Individual outer feathers may settle slightly.

The wings should behave like part of her body rather than decorative objects.

## Mood / Tone

Quiet.

Routine.

Slightly weary.

Warmly humorous without becoming a joke.

The audience should begin to understand that Madrigal has done this many,
many times before.

Cinematic medium shot of the winged young woman from the reference image
standing in a quiet snowy service alley beside a warmly lit restaurant just
before sunrise.

Preserve the character appearance, clothing, hairstyle, feathered wings,
body proportions, environment, lighting, colors, composition, and stylized

She has just finished landing and is preparing herself to go to work.

Her wings begin partially open behind her and slowly relax into a comfortable
resting position. The movement is gentle and anatomical. A few outer feathers
settle naturally.

She makes one small adjustment to her scarf, then naturally adjusts the strap
of the leather satchel resting on her shoulder.

She briefly brushes a little loose snow from the shoulder or front of her coat.

The gestures are simple, restrained, and sequential rather than simultaneous.

Afterward she becomes still and looks toward the nearby restaurant entrance.

She takes one small natural breath. Her shoulders rise and fall almost
imperceptibly.

Her expression is slightly tired but calm and capable, like someone mentally
preparing to begin another ordinary workday.

She does not speak.

Snow falls continuously throughout the scene at multiple depths. A few soft
flakes pass near the camera. Warm restaurant light spills onto the snow while
the surrounding street remains cool blue.

Her scarf, loose strands of hair, coat hem, satchel, and outer wing feathers
move subtly in the light winter breeze.

The camera remains almost stationary with only an extremely slow cinematic
push toward her.

Keep all movement restrained and natural.

No dramatic gestures. No walking. No flying. No exaggerated facial
expression. No additional characters. No objects appearing in her hands.
No dramatic wing movement.

Preserve her youthful stylized animated appearance and proportions throughout
the shot.

Quiet, intimate, painterly animated-film realism.

Approximately 5 seconds.

## Generation Notes

Watch specifically for:

- excessive or theatrical gestures
- both hands performing unrelated actions simultaneously
- malformed hands while adjusting the scarf
- satchel changing sides or disappearing
- wings moving dramatically
- extra wings or changing feather structure
- character becoming photorealistic
- character becoming younger, shorter, or childlike
- exaggerated sighing
- talking or lip movement
- restaurant architecture changing
- static background snowfall

The hand interaction with the scarf and satchel creates some generation risk.

If hand artifacts occur, simplify the action in this order:

1. Remove brushing snow from the coat.
2. Remove the scarf adjustment.
3. Keep only the satchel adjustment and breath.

The emotional beat is more important than completing every gesture.

## Production Notes

This is primarily a character-performance shot.

Do not judge it by how much happens.

A successful generation may contain only:

- wings settling
- one clothing adjustment
- a glance toward the restaurant
- one tired breath

The restraint is the point.

By the end of the shot, the audience should understand:

Madrigal isn't beginning an adventure.

She's beginning her shift.
