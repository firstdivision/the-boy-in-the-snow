# Shot 01E — Off to Work

## Status

Starting Frame

## Estimated Duration

~4 seconds

## Purpose

Complete Madrigal's arrival and transition from the quiet magical exterior
world into the busy restaurant kitchen.

Madrigal has finished preparing herself for work.

Now she simply goes inside.

## Composition

Medium-wide rear three-quarter shot.

Madrigal is walking toward the restaurant's service entrance.

Her folded wings are clearly visible against her back.

The warm service entrance occupies the destination side of the composition,
while the cold blue snowy alley remains visible behind her.

The composition should visually move Madrigal from:

    cold / blue / quiet
            ↓
    warm / gold / busy

## Time / Lighting

Predawn / early winter morning.

Cool blue ambient winter light dominates the alley.

Warm golden restaurant light spills from around the service entrance.

Continuous gentle snowfall.

## Camera

Camera remains behind and slightly to one side of Madrigal.

Either:

- nearly static as Madrigal walks away from camera, or
- an extremely subtle forward follow

Prefer the simpler static composition if it looks cinematic.

No orbit.

No dramatic push.

No camera passing around Madrigal to reveal her face.

## Action

Madrigal begins walking toward the restaurant service entrance.

Her pace is relaxed and purposeful.

She takes several natural steps through the snow.

Her wings remain folded comfortably against her back.

Her leather satchel moves subtly with each step.

Her coat hem and scarf respond naturally to her movement and the light winter
breeze.

Her boots compress the snow naturally as she walks.

Madrigal does not turn toward the camera.

She does not stop.

She is simply going to work.

## Character

Madrigal.

Maintain continuity with Shot 01D:

- youthful animated character design
- dark hair in loose messy bun
- dark blue-gray winter coat
- thick scarf
- winter boots
- brown leather work satchel
- large white feathered wings
- wings folded against her back
- normal young-adult proportions

## Wings

Wings remain folded throughout the shot.

They may respond very subtly to her walking motion.

No flapping.

No spreading.

No dramatic feather movement.

## Mood / Tone

Quiet and routine.

The beauty and magic of Madrigal's arrival has now become completely ordinary.

She's going through the employee entrance.

## Video Model

## Video Prompt

Cinematic medium-wide rear three-quarter shot of the winged young woman from
the reference image walking through a quiet snow-covered service alley toward
the warmly lit restaurant entrance.

Preserve the character appearance, clothing, hairstyle, folded feathered
wings, body proportions, environment, lighting, colors, composition, and
stylized animated-film aesthetic shown in the reference image.

She walks naturally and calmly toward the nearby service entrance at an
ordinary, relaxed pace. Her movement is purposeful but unhurried.

Her wings remain comfortably folded against her back throughout the shot.
They respond only very subtly to the natural movement of her body. No wing
flapping or spreading.

Her leather satchel swings gently with each step. Her coat hem, scarf, and
loose strands of hair move subtly as she walks and in the faint winter breeze.

Her boots step naturally into the existing soft snow with believable weight,
leaving subtle footprints.

The air is cold, clear, and still. There is snow already accumulated on the
ground, rooftops, steps, and surrounding buildings, but NO SNOW IS CURRENTLY
FALLING.

A small amount of chimney smoke may drift slowly in the distance.

Warm golden light from the restaurant entrance spills across the snow ahead
of her while the surrounding alley remains cool blue.

The camera stays behind and slightly to one side of her, remaining nearly
stationary with only a very subtle forward cinematic movement.

She continues toward the entrance for the entire shot.

No turning toward camera. No stopping. No flying. No spreading or flapping
wings. No dramatic gestures. No additional characters entering the scene.
No falling snow or airborne snowflakes.

Preserve her youthful stylized animated appearance and proportions throughout
the shot.

Quiet, restrained, painterly animated-film realism.

Approximately 4 seconds.

## Sound

The sound transition is especially important in this shot.

Exterior:

- soft winter wind
- footsteps compressing snow
- clothing movement
- subtle satchel movement
- very soft feather movement

Restaurant sounds become progressively more audible as she approaches:

- muffled conversation
- dishes
- pans
- kitchen equipment
- footsteps
- distant order calls

The kitchen should sound busy before we actually see it.

## Continuity

### From Shot 01D

Madrigal has:

- landed
- settled her wings
- adjusted herself
- looked toward the restaurant
- mentally prepared for work

Shot 01E begins with her moving toward the entrance.

Her wings are already folded.

Same alley, lighting, snowfall, restaurant, clothing, and character appearance.

### Into Scene 2

Restaurant sounds grow louder as Madrigal approaches the entrance.

The shot should end close enough to the service entrance that the audience
expects her to enter.

Do not necessarily show her opening the door.

Cut before or as she reaches it.

Scene 2 begins immediately inside the busy restaurant.

## Transition

The edit should use sound to bridge the two scenes.

Near the end of Shot 01E:

    quiet winter ambience
            +
    increasingly audible kitchen noise

Then:

    CUT TO INTERIOR

with the full kitchen soundscape suddenly present.

The contrast should make the restaurant feel hectic.

## Generation Notes

Watch specifically for:

- unnatural walking gait
- stiff legs
- sliding feet
- feet failing to compress/contact the snow
- wings beginning to flap
- wings changing shape
- satchel changing sides
- character turning toward camera
- character becoming photorealistic
- character becoming shorter or childlike
- restaurant architecture changing
- malformed hands
- additional people appearing
- frozen background snowfall

The walking motion is the priority.

Keep everything else simple.

If Kling introduces artifacts from too much secondary motion, remove details
rather than adding instructions.
