# Shot 01 — Kitchen Establishing

## Status

Starting Frame Approved

## Estimated Duration

~4 seconds

## Purpose

Establish the Copper Kettle kitchen during the morning breakfast rush.

The peaceful exterior of Scene 01 gives way immediately to a cramped,
noisy, hot, semi-organized chaos.

This shot establishes the kitchen geography, visual style, staff, and
head chef before Madrigal enters the scene.

## Story Beat

"The kitchen was semi-organized chaos as usual. Hot pans of sizzling
sausage and eggs covered the black stove, a faint acrid smoke filled
the air, and shouting was the only means of communication."

## Composition

Wide cinematic establishing shot of the Copper Kettle kitchen.

The kitchen is narrow, old, crowded, and functional.

A large black commercial stove and cooking line dominate the left side
of the frame.

A wooden preparation counter occupies the right.

The central aisle creates depth through the kitchen.

Multiple cooks work at different stations.

The head chef is visible near the center-left of the composition,
already instructing one of his junior chefs.

He should be recognizable enough that the audience can identify him
when we move closer in later shots.

Madrigal is NOT visible in this shot.

## Kitchen Design

This image establishes the canonical Copper Kettle kitchen.

Important visual elements:

- old Copper Creek architecture
- narrow, crowded working kitchen
- dark wood beams
- aged cream-colored tile
- dark iron cooking equipment
- black stove / cooking line
- worn wooden prep surfaces
- copper pots and pans
- hanging cookware
- open shelving
- stacks of plates
- bowls of ingredients
- handwritten breakfast orders
- practical pendant lighting
- warm service area
- visible steam and cooking haze

The kitchen should feel old and well-used rather than dirty.

Everything has a purpose.

## Color Palette

Maintain the established film palette.

Interior:

- warm amber and gold practical lighting
- deep brown wood
- dark iron
- aged copper
- muted cream tile
- warm food tones

Exterior light entering through windows:

- cool predawn blue

The combination of cool blue exterior light and warm amber interior
light should visually connect this location to Scene 01.

Avoid bright white modern restaurant lighting.

## Visual Style

Painterly animated-film aesthetic.

Characters should be clearly stylized rather than photorealistic.

Use:

- simplified animated facial features
- expressive silhouettes
- painterly surfaces
- soft edges
- warm cinematic lighting
- slightly exaggerated character proportions
- rich environmental detail

The environment may contain more texture and detail than the characters.

Do NOT drift toward live-action or photographic realism.

## Action

The kitchen is already fully active.

Several independent but simple actions occur throughout the frame.

A cook works over pans of eggs and sausage on the stove.

Another cook prepares food at a workstation.

A server or kitchen worker moves through the background carrying plates.

The head chef talks emphatically to a junior chef.

Steam rises continuously from the stove.

A faint cooking haze hangs in the warm air.

The activity should feel busy without becoming frantic slapstick.

## Head Chef

This shot introduces the chef involved in Madrigal's first appointment.

He is:

- middle-aged
- stocky
- experienced
- authoritative
- expressive
- slightly intimidating
- dressed in traditional kitchen whites
- wearing a tall white chef's hat
- wearing a dark apron

He is accustomed to being in charge.

He communicates loudly because everyone in this kitchen communicates loudly.

His appearance should remain consistent throughout Scene 02.

## Madrigal

Madrigal does not appear in Shot 01.

Her absence is intentional.

The audience first experiences the kitchen as the restaurant staff experience
it: an ordinary chaotic breakfast shift.

## Camera

Wide establishing shot.

Camera is approximately eye level.

Use a very slow cinematic push into the kitchen.

The movement should be subtle.

Do not pan rapidly around the room.

Do not follow individual characters.

The audience should have time to absorb the environment.

## Video Prompt

Wide cinematic establishing shot of the busy animated restaurant kitchen
shown in the reference image during an early morning breakfast rush.

Preserve the kitchen architecture, composition, stove, counters, hanging
cookware, characters, lighting, colors, and stylized painterly animated-film
appearance shown in the reference image.

The kitchen is alive with restrained, natural activity.

The cook at the stove tends sizzling pans of breakfast food with small,
purposeful movements.

Steam continuously rises from several hot pans and pots and slowly moves
through the warm kitchen air.

The large head chef near the center speaks emphatically to the junior cook
beside him, using one restrained hand gesture while the junior cook listens.

Other kitchen workers continue their tasks in the background. One carries
plates through the work area while others prepare food at their stations.

Movements should overlap naturally so the kitchen feels busy and inhabited,
but each person should perform only simple believable actions.

A faint cooking haze hangs in the room.

Warm pendant lights remain steady while cool predawn light enters subtly
through the windows.

The camera performs a very slow, smooth cinematic push deeper into the
kitchen.

Preserve the clearly stylized animated appearance of every character.
Do not make the people photorealistic or live-action.

No dramatic camera movement. No exaggerated gestures. No slapstick.
No collisions. No falling objects. No new foreground characters.
No winged character appears in this shot.

Keep the kitchen layout and character appearances stable throughout the shot.

Warm, busy, slightly smoky, painterly animated-film atmosphere.

Approximately 4 seconds.

## Sound

This shot introduces the Copper Kettle kitchen soundscape.

The transition from Scene 01 should be immediate and noticeable.

Primary sounds:

- sausage sizzling
- eggs frying
- metal pans
- plates and dishes
- utensils
- footsteps
- stove sounds
- kitchen ventilation
- overlapping voices
- cooks calling to one another

Background:

- muffled dining-room activity
- occasional plate placed on service counter
- subtle room tone

The kitchen should sound busy and somewhat overwhelming.

Do not make it cartoonishly loud.

## Music

Music from Scene 01 may continue briefly across the cut.

Consider reducing or temporarily dropping the score after entering the
kitchen so the dense natural kitchen soundscape dominates.

This contrast helps establish Madrigal's working environment.

## Continuity

### From Scene 01

Scene 01 ends outside the restaurant.

The exterior is:

- cold
- blue
- quiet
- still

Shot 01 cuts directly inside to:

- warm
- amber
- crowded
- noisy
- active

The contrast should be immediate.

### Into Shot 02

Shot 02 introduces Madrigal entering or hurrying into the kitchen.

The kitchen activity established here continues uninterrupted.

The head chef and junior cooks remain occupied with breakfast service.

## Transition

Hard cut from the quiet exterior.

Prefer a sound-driven transition.

The first frame of the kitchen may arrive with a strong combination of:

    SIZZLE
    CLATTER
    VOICES

This should make the audience immediately feel the difference between
Madrigal's peaceful flight through Copper Creek and the environment in
which she actually works.

## Generation Notes

Watch specifically for:

- characters becoming photorealistic
- faces changing during the shot
- extra arms or hands
- cookware appearing or disappearing
- excessive character motion
- people colliding
- exaggerated shouting animation
- camera movement becoming too aggressive
- kitchen architecture changing
- excessive smoke obscuring the scene
- modern kitchen elements appearing
- text/signage mutating noticeably

Do not ask the model to perform too many complicated actions.

The impression of chaos should come from many SMALL motions occurring
simultaneously rather than large dramatic actions.

Steam, sizzling food, moving workers, dishes, gestures, and sound will
create most of the energy.

## Production Notes

This shot serves two purposes:

1. Establish the emotional contrast between the peaceful exterior and
   Madrigal's working environment.

2. Establish a reusable visual reference for the Copper Kettle kitchen.

The kitchen should feel like a real workplace where everyone has developed
their own rhythm within the chaos.

It is not disorder.

It is semi-organized chaos.
