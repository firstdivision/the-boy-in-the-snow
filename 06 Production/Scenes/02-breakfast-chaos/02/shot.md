# Shot 02 — Madrigal Arrives

## Status

Starting Frame Needed

## Estimated Duration

~4 seconds

## Purpose

Bring Madrigal into the Copper Kettle kitchen and establish that she has
arrived only moments before she is needed.

The kitchen continues operating around her without acknowledging her presence.

This begins to establish an important rule of Madrigal's world:

The people she protects do not consciously see her.

## Story Beat

"The day started with a chef at a restaurant. Madrigal nearly missed this
first appointment but arrived just in time."

## Composition

Medium-wide shot inside the Copper Kettle kitchen.

Madrigal has just entered from the service entrance and is moving into the
busy kitchen.

Frame her from a front or front three-quarter angle so her face and slightly
hurried expression are visible.

The kitchen remains active around her.

Workers occupy the foreground and background, creating depth and movement.

The head chef may be visible deeper in the kitchen, but he is not yet the
primary subject.

## Environment

Maintain the Copper Kettle kitchen established in Shot 01:

- narrow old kitchen
- black stove / cooking line
- wooden prep surfaces
- aged cream tile
- copper cookware
- dark wood
- hanging pots and pans
- warm amber practical lighting
- cool predawn light through windows
- steam and faint cooking haze
- busy breakfast service

The kitchen must feel like the same physical location as Shot 01.

## Character — Madrigal

Maintain Madrigal's established appearance:

- youthful stylized animated facial design
- dark hair loosely gathered into a messy bun
- dark blue-gray winter coat
- thick scarf
- winter boots
- brown leather work satchel
- large white feathered wings
- normal young-adult proportions

Her wings are folded closely against her back while indoors.

She should remain clearly stylized and animated rather than photorealistic.

## Action

Madrigal enters the kitchen with slightly hurried energy.

She moves several steps into the room while looking across the kitchen for
her appointment.

Her pace communicates:

    I'm late.
    Where is he?
    There.

She spots the head chef.

Her attention immediately settles on him.

Her expression changes subtly from hurried concern to focused attention.

She does not speak.

Kitchen workers continue moving naturally around her.

Nobody looks at Madrigal.

Nobody reacts to her wings.

One worker may pass surprisingly close to her without acknowledging her,
subtly reinforcing that she is invisible to them.

## Performance

Madrigal should look mildly rushed rather than panicked.

She is a professional who has arrived later than she intended.

Possible subtle details:

- slightly quicker breathing
- coat still settling from her hurried arrival
- satchel shifting against her side
- a quick scan of the room
- small moment of relief when she locates the chef

Avoid exaggerated panting, running, or comedic lateness.

## Wings

Her wings remain folded.

They move only slightly with her body.

No spreading.

No flapping.

No dramatic feather movement.

## Camera

Medium-wide cinematic shot.

Camera may track backward very slightly as Madrigal moves into the kitchen,
or remain mostly stationary and allow her to move through the composition.

Prefer simple, stable movement.

No orbit.

No rapid pan.

No dramatic zoom.

## Video Prompt

Cinematic medium-wide shot inside the busy animated restaurant kitchen shown

Preserve the young winged woman's appearance, clothing, hairstyle, folded
feathered wings, body proportions, kitchen architecture, lighting, colors,
and stylized painterly animated-film aesthetic shown in the reference image.

The young woman moves several purposeful steps farther into the busy kitchen.
She has just arrived and appears slightly hurried, as though she is almost
late for something important.

Her wings remain comfortably folded against her back.

Her leather satchel moves naturally against her side as she walks. Her coat
and scarf settle subtly from her recent movement.

She quickly scans the busy kitchen, looking from one side of the room to the
other.

Then she notices the head chef working deeper in the kitchen.

Her gaze settles on him and her expression changes subtly from mild concern
to focused attention, with a tiny sense of relief that she arrived in time.

She does not speak.

The restaurant workers continue their normal breakfast tasks around her
without looking at her or reacting to her presence.

One kitchen worker passes naturally near her while carrying something,
without acknowledging her.

Background activity remains restrained and believable: cooks work at their
stations, pans sizzle, steam rises, plates move through the kitchen, and the
head chef continues talking to junior cooks.

Keep individual actions simple.

The camera remains stable with only a very subtle cinematic movement.

No dramatic gestures. No running. No collisions. No flying. No wing
spreading. No character looks directly at the camera. No additional winged
characters.

Preserve the young woman's youthful stylized animated appearance throughout
the shot. Do not make her photorealistic or live-action.

Warm, busy, painterly animated-film atmosphere.

Approximately 4 seconds.

## Transition

Cut from the kitchen establishing shot to Madrigal already inside the kitchen.

Do not show her opening the service door.

This avoids unnecessary hand/door interaction and keeps the sequence moving.

At the end of Shot 02, Madrigal looks toward the chef.

Cut on her eyeline to Shot 03.

## Generation Notes

Watch specifically for:

- Madrigal becoming photorealistic
- Madrigal becoming too young or childlike
- wings changing size or shape
- wings opening unexpectedly
- satchel disappearing or changing sides
- malformed walking motion
- kitchen workers reacting to Madrigal
- kitchen workers staring at her
- Madrigal colliding with another worker
- excessive facial animation
- exaggerated running
- kitchen architecture changing
- head chef changing appearance
- extra winged characters

The important performance beat is extremely simple:

    enter slightly hurried
            ↓
    scan room
            ↓
    spot chef
            ↓
    focus

If complicated background movement causes generation problems, simplify the
workers before simplifying Madrigal's performance.

## Production Notes

This shot should quietly establish Madrigal's invisibility rather than
explicitly explaining it.

Nobody needs to walk through her or perform anything supernatural.

A cook simply passing close beside a woman with enormous white wings without
even glancing at her is enough.

The audience will understand.

The final look toward the chef motivates the cut into Shot 03 and begins the
actual appointment.
