# Shot 07 — The Knife Falls

## Status

Starting Frame Needed

## Estimated Duration

~2 seconds

## Purpose

Complete Madrigal's intervention.

The knife has been redirected safely away from the chef's fingertip, but the
unexpected sideways movement causes it to slip from his hand.

The chef is completely unharmed.

His immediate reaction, however, is annoyance rather than gratitude.

## Story Beat

"Her intervention caused the knife to come out of the chef's hand who then
cursed loudly, oblivious to the wound he had narrowly escaped."

## Composition

Medium-close shot of the chef at the preparation counter.

The framing is wider than Shot 06 so we can see:

- the chef
- his hands
- the cutting board
- the partially chopped onion
- the knife coming loose
- enough of the surrounding kitchen to restore spatial context

Madrigal may be visible at one side of the frame after completing her
intervention.

Her hand is withdrawing from the knife.

`starting-image.png`

## Starting Image Requirements

Begin immediately after Shot 06.

The onion is already partially chopped.

A pile of chopped onion remains on the cutting board.

The chef's holding hand and fingertips are completely unharmed.

The knife has been redirected away from his fingers and is beginning to slip
from his grip.

Madrigal is close beside the preparation area, withdrawing the hand she used
to redirect the knife.

Her expression is still intensely focused.

Maintain the same:

- chef
- clothing
- knife
- cutting board
- partially chopped onion
- kitchen
- Madrigal design
- warm amber lighting
- painterly animated-film style

as Shot 06.

## Action

The knife slips completely out of the chef's hand.

It falls a short distance and lands harmlessly on the wooden preparation
counter.

    CLATTER!

The knife does NOT fly dramatically across the room.

It does NOT spin through the air.

It does NOT land blade-first.

It simply slips away and falls flat onto the work surface.

The chef instinctively jerks his hand backward.

He looks down at the fallen knife in surprise.

Then irritation immediately replaces surprise.

He begins to curse.

Madrigal realizes the rescue worked.

For one brief moment, her tense expression changes into a small,
triumphant smile.

She did it.

CUT before the chef's angry reaction fully develops.

## Chef Performance

The chef does not understand why the knife slipped.

His reaction progresses quickly:

    surprise
       ↓
    confusion
       ↓
    irritation

He does NOT inspect his fingertip.

He does NOT realize he was almost injured.

From his perspective, his knife simply slipped out of his hand during an
already frustrating breakfast rush.

## Madrigal Performance

Madrigal's urgency disappears the instant she sees that the chef is safe.

She looks at:

    chef's hand
        ↓
    safe fingertip
        ↓
    fallen knife

Then she allows herself a small smile.

It is genuine satisfaction.

For perhaps half a second she feels:

    I got there in time.

This smile is important because Shot 08 will take it away.

## Camera

Mostly stationary medium-close shot.

No dramatic camera movement.

A tiny handheld-like settling motion is acceptable when the knife lands,
but avoid actual camera shake.

The knife landing should remain clearly visible.

## Video Prompt

Cinematic animated kitchen shot continuing directly from the reference image.

Preserve the young winged woman's appearance, the chef's appearance, clothing,
hands, knife, partially chopped onion, chopped onion pieces, wooden cutting
board, kitchen lighting, colors, composition, and painterly animated-film
aesthetic shown in the reference image.

The chef's knife has just been redirected safely away from his fingers.

His fingers remain completely unharmed.

The knife slips naturally from his hand and falls only a short distance onto
the wooden preparation counter, landing harmlessly with a simple clatter.

The knife does not fly across the room and does not spin dramatically.

The chef instinctively pulls his hand back and looks down at the fallen knife
with brief surprise. His surprise quickly begins turning into irritation.

Beside him, the young winged woman withdraws her hand after the successful
intervention.

She quickly looks at the chef's unharmed fingers, realizes that the danger
has passed, and her tense expression relaxes.

A small, genuine, triumphant smile appears on her face.

Her wings remain folded behind her.

Keep all movement restrained and physically believable.

No injury. No blood. No wound. No knife throwing. No spinning knife.
No dramatic action. No magical effects. No wing spreading.

Preserve the stylized animated appearance of all characters.
Do not make them photorealistic or live-action.

## Sound

Kitchen ambience continues.

Foreground:

    CLATTER!

as the knife lands on the wooden counter.

The chef gives a startled grunt or sharp intake of breath.

Immediately afterward he begins an angry vocal reaction.

The full curse can carry across the cut into Shot 08.

## Dialogue

The final dialogue should be recorded separately.

The chef begins his outburst near the end of this shot.

For example:

    CHEF
    Goddammit!

The precise wording can be decided during sound editing.

## Music

The tension releases immediately when the knife misses his finger.

There may be a tiny musical release corresponding with Madrigal's smile.

Do not make it heroic.

The audience should have just enough time to share Madrigal's satisfaction
before the chef ruins the moment.

## Continuity

### From Shot 06

Shot 06 ends as the knife begins slipping from the chef's hand.

Shot 07 begins at that exact moment.

The onion must remain partially chopped.

The chopped onion pile, chef's hand position, knife, cutting board, and
Madrigal's position should closely match Shot 06.

### Into Shot 08

Madrigal ends this shot with a small triumphant smile.

The chef begins cursing.

Shot 08 stays with Madrigal as she hears his angry outburst.

Her smile disappears.

This creates the emotional reversal:

    relief
       ↓
    pride
       ↓
    chef curses
       ↓
    hurt

## Transition

Fast cut from Shot 06.

Let the knife clatter provide the punctuation.

At the end of Shot 07, begin the chef's curse before cutting to Madrigal.

Carry his voice across the edit into Shot 08.

This sound bridge makes his reaction feel immediate and lets Shot 08 focus
entirely on Madrigal's face.

## Generation Notes

The knife movement should be extremely simple.

Watch for:

- knife flying across the room
- knife spinning
- knife changing shape
- knife sticking upright in the counter
- knife falling toward someone's body
- malformed chef hands
- injured fingers
- Madrigal touching the blade
- Madrigal grabbing the knife
- exaggerated chef reaction
- Madrigal opening her wings
- photorealistic style drift

If Kling struggles with the falling knife, the usable generated action only
needs to be:

    knife leaves hand
          ↓
    knife drops

The actual CLATTER can sell the landing in Resolve.
