# Shot 09 — A Tired Child With a Blanket

## Status

Starting Frame Needed

## Estimated Duration

~6–8 seconds

## Purpose

Show Madrigal quietly comforting herself after the chef's angry reaction.

Her wings become more than a means of flight. They are part of her emotional
body language and something she instinctively uses for comfort.

## Story Beat

"She wrapped her wings around her for comfort like a tired child with a
blanket."

## Composition

Medium shot of Madrigal standing near the edge of the busy kitchen.

She is isolated visually even though people continue working around her.

Her face, upper body, and most of both wings should be visible so we can
clearly see the wings move around her.

The chef should be absent or soft and distant in the background.

## Starting Image

`starting-image.png`

Madrigal stands quietly with her shoulders slightly lowered after the events
of Shot 08.

Her expression is disappointed and tired.

Her gaze is lowered.

Her large white wings are still mostly folded behind her but relaxed enough
that they can naturally begin moving forward.

Maintain her established:

- youthful animated character design
- messy dark hair bun
- dark blue-gray winter coat
- thick scarf
- brown leather satchel
- large white feathered wings
- normal young-adult proportions

Maintain the warm amber Copper Kettle kitchen and painterly animated style.

## Action

Madrigal remains still for a brief moment.

The kitchen continues moving around her.

She exhales softly.

Her shoulders relax.

Then her wings slowly loosen from behind her.

Both wings move gently forward around her shoulders and sides, enclosing her
in a loose protective embrace.

The movement should resemble someone quietly pulling a warm blanket around
themselves after a difficult moment.

Madrigal slightly settles into the feathers.

Her expression remains tired and disappointed, but the wings provide a small
measure of comfort.

She stays like this for a moment.

No one notices.

## Performance

Keep everything restrained.

Madrigal is NOT:

- crying
- devastated
- dramatically collapsing
- pouting
- angry

She has simply experienced another small disappointment in a job where no
one knows what she does.

There should be a sense that this little self-comforting gesture is familiar
to her.

## Wings

The wings are the primary performance element.

Movement should be:

- slow
- gentle
- symmetrical enough to feel intentional
- physically connected to her back
- soft and protective

They do NOT flap.

They do NOT lift her.

They do NOT become dramatically larger.

They simply curl forward around her body.

## Camera

Medium cinematic shot.

Very slow subtle push toward Madrigal.

No pan.
No orbit.
No dramatic movement.

## Video Model

## Video Prompt

Cinematic medium shot of the young winged woman from the reference image
standing quietly inside the warm busy animated restaurant kitchen.

Preserve her exact appearance, hairstyle, clothing, satchel, body
proportions, large white feathered wings, kitchen lighting, colors, and
painterly animated-film style.

She stands with her gaze lowered and shoulders slightly slumped, quietly
disappointed and tired.

She pauses and gives a small natural exhale.

Then her large folded wings slowly and gently move forward from behind her,
curling around her shoulders and sides.

The movement feels like a tired person pulling a soft blanket around
themselves for comfort.

She settles slightly into the feathers and remains quietly wrapped in her
wings for a moment.

Her expression softens slightly but remains tired and thoughtful.

She does not cry, speak, or make any dramatic gesture.

Background cooks continue their normal work without looking at her or
acknowledging her presence.

The wings move gently and naturally. No flapping, flying, sudden spreading,
or dramatic feather movement.

Camera performs only a very slow subtle push toward her.

Preserve the stylized painterly animated appearance. Do not become
photorealistic.

Quiet, intimate, restrained emotional moment.

Duration: 6–8 seconds.

## Sound

- sizzling
- dishes
- distant voices
- pans
- ventilation

The chef may still be faintly muttering somewhere behind her.

Madrigal gives one quiet exhale.

Add subtle feather movement as the wings close around her.

The kitchen does not stop for her.

## Music

Avoid overt sadness.

The music should communicate tenderness more than misery.
