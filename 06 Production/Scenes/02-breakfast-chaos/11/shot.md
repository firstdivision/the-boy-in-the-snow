# Shot 11 — No Time to Wallow

## Status

Starting Frame Needed

## Estimated Duration

~5–7 seconds

## Purpose

Move Madrigal out of her disappointment and toward her next appointment.

She cannot stay and dwell on what happened.

Someone else needs her.

## Story Beat

"There was no time to wallow for Madrigal, there was always another job
to do. She ran outside leaving the busy kitchen behind..."

## Composition

Medium-wide shot inside the Copper Kettle kitchen.

Madrigal begins near the preparation area where we left her in Shot 10.

The service exit is visible farther behind or to one side of her.

The chef remains at his station chopping the onion.

Other cooks continue working.

The kitchen should feel busy and indifferent to Madrigal's departure.

## Starting Image

`starting-image.png`

Madrigal has just looked toward the service exit.

Her wings are still partly wrapped around her but have begun relaxing.

Her posture has straightened slightly.

Her expression is changing from tired resignation to renewed purpose.

The chef remains at his counter in the background, focused entirely on the
half-cut onion.

## Action

Madrigal looks toward the exit.

A brief realization crosses her face:

    I'm late.

Her wings gently unwrap from around her body and fold naturally behind her.

Her shoulders straighten.

The tiredness is still there, but purpose takes over.

She turns toward the service exit.

Then she moves quickly through the kitchen.

She begins with a brisk walk that becomes a hurried jog as she approaches
the door.

The cooks continue working around her without noticing her.

The chef keeps chopping.

Madrigal reaches the service exit and pushes through the door.

CUT as she disappears outside.

## Performance

The change should be subtle but clear:

    disappointment
         ↓
    remembers next appointment
         ↓
    purpose returns
         ↓
    GO

She does not suddenly become cheerful.

She simply has a job to do.

## Wings

Her wings begin partly wrapped around her.

They gently unwrap and return to their normal folded position against her
back BEFORE she starts running.

Once folded, keep them folded for the rest of the shot.

No flapping.

No flight.

## Chef

The chef continues chopping the onion.

IMPORTANT:

He cannot see Madrigal.

He never looks toward her.

He never reacts to her leaving.

He remains focused on his work.

## Camera

Begin with a medium-wide composition.

As Madrigal turns and moves toward the exit, the camera may gently pan or
track with her.

Keep the movement smooth and restrained.

Do not make this feel like an action sequence.

## Video Model

Kling 3.0 Pro

## Video Prompt

Cinematic animated shot continuing inside the warm busy restaurant kitchen.

Preserve the young winged woman's appearance, clothing, satchel, wings,
proportions, kitchen lighting, colors, and painterly animated-film style.

She looks toward the service exit and suddenly remembers she must leave.

Her tired expression becomes focused and purposeful. Her white wings gently
unwrap from around her body and fold naturally against her back.

She straightens, turns toward the exit, and moves quickly through the busy
kitchen, beginning with a brisk walk and becoming a hurried jog.

Background cooks continue working normally and never acknowledge her.

The bearded head chef remains at his preparation counter, looking down and
chopping the half-cut onion. He never looks toward the young woman and never
reacts to her.

She reaches the service door, opens it, and hurries outside.

Her wings remain folded while she moves. No flying or wing flapping.

Camera follows her smoothly with restrained cinematic movement.

Preserve the stylized painterly animated appearance. Do not become
photorealistic.

Duration: 5–7 seconds.

A little momentum returns.

Not heroic or triumphant.

Madrigal simply has somewhere else to be.
